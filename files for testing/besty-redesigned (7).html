Our Story

Started With One Smash.
Never Looked Back.

Besty's didn't open with a business plan — it opened with a griddle, a stubborn refusal to serve a soft crust, and a bet that Kondhwa deserved a burger worth queuing for. Since [YEAR], what started as a one-man obsession with getting the smash right has turned into a neighborhood staple, built one patty, one late night, one sold-out weekend at a time. We're still doing it exactly the way we did on day one: fresh, loud, and completely unwilling to cut a corner.

What We Stand For

Our Values

No Gimmicks

Real ingredients, honestly cooked. No freezer-to-fryer shortcuts, no flavor tricks — just meat, fire, and technique.

Smashed Fresh, Daily

Patties are smashed to order, never pre-formed or frozen. If it's not fresh off the griddle, it's not going out.

Built For Kondhwa

We're not a chain pretending to be local. This is our neighborhood, our regulars, our home turf.

Zero Shortcuts

From the bun toast to the last drizzle of sauce, every step gets the same care as the first burger we ever made.

Our Mission

To smash out the best burger in Pune, one honest ingredient at a time — no gimmicks, no shortcuts, just serious flavor served fast.

Our Vision

To be the name Pune reaches for first when "burger" comes up — known not just for the hype, but for consistently backing it up.
