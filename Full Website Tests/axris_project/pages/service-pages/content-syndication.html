· Syndication Features

Precision distribution.

01

Multi-Channel Network

We promote your assets across a curated network of B2B newsletters, publisher networks, and targeted digital display properties.

02

Fixed Cost-Per-Lead

Remove the guesswork from your marketing budget. Agree to a flat CPL upfront and only pay for leads that match your exact criteria.

03

Custom Qualification

Add BANT questions or custom fields to the download forms to ensure prospects have current projects or immediate pain points.

04

ABM Alignment

Provide your target account list (TAL). We will suppress irrelevant companies and ensure your content only reaches active prospects within your key accounts.

05

100% Data Scrubbing

Every downloaded lead goes through a rigorous manual and automated QA process to eliminate generic emails, test inputs, and competitors.

06

GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Full legal compliance is built-in. Every generated lead provides explicit, documented consent to be contacted by your sales team.

· The Workflow

How we syndicate.

01

Asset Selection & Targeting

We analyze your content library (eBooks, whitepapers, webinars), select the strongest assets, and map out the exact job titles, industries, and company sizes we will target.

02

Network Amplification

Your content is gated and published across our specialized B2B publisher network. Prospects discover your asset, fill out the qualification form, and download the content.

03

Verification & Handoff

We scrub the downloaded data for accuracy, ensuring it matches your ICP filters. Validated leads are formatted and injected directly into your marketing automation platform.

  • Zero duplicates—automatically cross-referenced against your CRM
