· Our Capabilities

Comprehensive data hygiene.

01

CRM Deduplication

We merge and purge conflicting records, utilizing advanced fuzzy-matching logic to resolve duplicated accounts and contacts without losing historical activity data.

02

Email Verification

Protect your sender reputation. We ping every email address in your database to identify hard bounces, spam traps, and catch-all servers before you hit send.

03

Firmographic Enrichment

We fill in the blanks on your accounts. Appending company size, annual revenue, industry NAICS codes, and active technology stacks to enable better ABM segmentation.

04

Contact Appending

Give your SDRs the tools they need. We locate and append missing direct dial phone numbers, mobile numbers, and updated LinkedIn profiles to your existing contacts.

05

Data Standardization

We normalize job titles (e.g., turning "VP of Mktg" to "Vice President of Marketing"), format addresses, and structure phone numbers to ensure clean CRM reporting.

06

Compliance Scrubbing

We cross-reference your database against regional compliance registries (GDPR, CCPA, Do-Not-Call lists) to ensure your outreach remains legally protected.

· The Process

How we fix it.

01

Audit & Assessment

We ingest a secure sample of your CRM data to run a diagnostic health check, identifying duplicate rates, decay levels, and missing critical fields.

02

Cleansing & Enrichment

Using a combination of automated API waterfalls and manual research teams, we scrub the invalid records and layer on over 20+ points of demographic and firmographic enrichment.

03

Handoff & Activation

The pristine data is returned to you via secure CSV or injected directly back into your CRM. We provide a full change-log report so you know exactly what was updated.
