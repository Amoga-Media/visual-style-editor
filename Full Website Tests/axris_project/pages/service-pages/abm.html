· ABM Architecture

Precision at every tier.

01

Target Account Selection

We utilize predictive analytics, intent data, and historical sales performance to build a high-propensity Target Account List (TAL) tailored to your goals.

02

Buying Committee Mapping

Enterprise deals require consensus. We identify and map out the entire buying committee—champions, influencers, and decision-makers—within each target account.

03

Hyper-Personalized Content

We create 1:1, 1:Few, and 1:Many content experiences, tailoring ads, landing pages, and email copy to specific account pain points and industry verticals.

04

Multi-Channel Orchestration

Surround your accounts wherever they are. We coordinate touchpoints across IP-targeted display ads, LinkedIn targeting, email nurturing, and direct mail.

05

Sales & Marketing Alignment

ABM doesn't work in silos. We facilitate tight alignment with your sales team, creating unified "Account Playbooks" to ensure a seamless handoff.

06

Account-Level Analytics

We shift the focus from individual lead metrics to account engagement scores, tracking the collective interaction of the entire buying committee over time.

· The Workflow

How we orchestrate.

01

Account Intelligence Gathering

Before we launch a single ad, we conduct deep research on your Tier 1 and Tier 2 accounts, identifying their tech stacks, recent news, pain points, and key stakeholders.

02

Omni-Channel Activation

We deploy synchronized campaigns that "surround" the account. A decision-maker will see a targeted LinkedIn ad, receive a personalized email, and visit a custom landing page built just for their company.

03

Pipeline Velocity Tracking

As accounts engage with our assets, their intent score rises. Once they cross a predefined threshold, sales is immediately alerted with actionable insights for a warm outreach motion.
