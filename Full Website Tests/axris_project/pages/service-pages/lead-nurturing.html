· What's Included

Automate your relationship building.

01

Buyer Journey Mapping

We break down your sales cycle into distinct phases (Awareness, Consideration, Decision) and map out the exact touchpoints required to move prospects forward.

02

Multi-Channel Sequences

Go beyond just email. We integrate LinkedIn touches, SMS where applicable, and retargeting ads to surround your prospect with a cohesive narrative.

03

Value-Driven Content

We leverage your existing assets (case studies, webinars, whitepapers) or help craft new ones to provide undeniable value in every interaction.

04

Behavioral Lead Scoring

Assign point values to actions (opening an email, visiting pricing pages). When a prospect hits a threshold, they are automatically routed to your sales team.

05

Time-Based Drip Campaigns

Keep your brand relevant for long-cycle deals with gentle, periodic check-ins that prevent prospects from forgetting about you over months or years.

06

CRM & Tech Integration

We build these systems directly into your existing infrastructure—whether that's HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Salesforce, or Marketo—for seamless data flow.

How we execute.
