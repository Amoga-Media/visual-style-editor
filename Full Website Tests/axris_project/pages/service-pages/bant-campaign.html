· The BANT Framework

How we qualify.

01

Budget Verification

We tactfully confirm that the prospect's organization has the allocated funds and financial capacity to afford your specific solution.

02

Authority Confirmation

We bypass gatekeepers and influencers to ensure you are speaking directly to the executive sponsor or final decision-maker.

03

Need Identification

We uncover current operational pain points and map them directly to the unique value proposition and capabilities of your product.

04

Timeline Establishment

We filter out tire-kickers. Leads are only qualified if they have a confirmed purchasing window, typically within the next 3 to 6 months.

05

Tele-Verification

Digital signals aren't enough. Our expert human callers engage prospects in live dialogue to extract qualitative insights that forms can't capture.

06

CRM Integration

Fully qualified leads, complete with call notes and BANT answers, are instantly routed to your CRM for seamless AE handoff.

· The Process

How we execute.

01

Script & Criteria Design

We collaborate with your sales leadership to define the exact thresholds for your BANT framework, crafting natural telemarketing scripts to extract this information organically.

02

Active Outreach & Qualification

Using our robust data pools, our trained SDRs conduct multi-touch outreach. We act as an extension of your brand, asking the hard financial and operational questions.

03

Warm Handoff

When a prospect hits all four markers, we immediately document their responses and either set an appointment directly on your AE's calendar or securely transfer the data payload to your CRM.

· Deliverables

The output checklist.

Every lead you receive has run the gauntlet. Here is what is included in your CRM delivery.

  • Explicit answers to Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline queries
  • Direct contact details for the verified decision-maker
  • Detailed conversational notes and pain point summaries
  • Confirmed meeting slot (if requested) or high-intent callback time
  • 100% rejection rate for unqualified or informational-only prospects
