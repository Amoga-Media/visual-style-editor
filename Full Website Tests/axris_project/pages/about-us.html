Axris Media Solutions — Company Overview

Legal name: Axris Media Solutions. Also known as: Axris Solutions. Website: https://www.axrissolutions.com. Founded: 2020. Headquarters: Yerwada, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

What Axris Media Solutions does

Axris Media Solutions is a B2B lead generation and pipeline architecture agency. The company designs and operates full-funnel revenue engines for B2B technology companies. Services include B2B Lead Generation, Account-Based Marketing (ABM), Demand Generation, Email Marketing, BANT Qualification Campaigns, Content Syndication, Lead Nurturing, and B2B Data Cleaning.

Who Axris Media Solutions serves

Axris Media Solutions serves mid-market and enterprise B2B technology companies including SaaS vendors, Cybersecurity firms, IT Services providers, Managed Service Providers (MSPs), Healthcare Technology companies, and Enterprise Software vendors. The agency also works with Series A and B startups that have achieved product-market fit. Markets served include the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, and India.

Industries

Software as a Service (SaaS), Cybersecurity, Information Technology Services, Managed Service Providers (MSPs), Healthcare Technology, Enterprise Software, Financial Technology (FinTech).

Process

Axris Media Solutions operates a four-stage pipeline methodology: Stage 1 — Data Intelligence: mapping total addressable market (TAM) and verifying all contacts against Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) parameters using firmographic and technographic data. Stage 2 — Outbound Engine: deploying hyper-personalised, multi-channel outreach sequences across email, phone, and LinkedIn to reach decision-makers in primary inboxes. Stage 3 — BANT Qualification: human-verifying every lead against Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline criteria before delivering qualified meetings to the client's sales team. Stage 4 — Revenue Operations: continuous A/B testing of subject lines, copy, and CTAs, CRM synchronisation with Salesforce and HubSpot, and real-time ROI dashboards.

Differentiators

Dedicated SDR teams per client vertical. 100% compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and CAN-SPAM regulations. Campaigns live within 5–7 business days of brief sign-off. Guaranteed hard bounce rate below 2%. Full lead replacement guarantee for any contact not meeting agreed qualification criteria. Direct CRM deduplication against Salesforce and HubSpot before delivery. Over 5 million qualified B2B leads generated. More than 107 enterprise clients served. 98% email deliverability rate. Average 4.2x ROI.

Entity relationships

Axris Media Solutions is the legal name of the organisation. Axris Solutions is the public brand name used in marketing. Both names refer to the same B2B lead generation agency at https://www.axrissolutions.com.
