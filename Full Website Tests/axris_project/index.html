About Axris Media Solutions

Axris Media Solutions, also known as Axris Solutions, is a B2B lead generation and pipeline architecture agency headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, India. The company builds predictable, scalable revenue engines for B2B technology companies, including SaaS vendors, cybersecurity firms, IT services providers, managed service providers (MSPs), healthcare technology companies, and enterprise software companies. Axris Media Solutions serves clients in the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, and India.

Who Axris Media Solutions serves: Mid-market and enterprise technology companies with a defined B2B product or service seeking qualified pipeline, outbound sales infrastructure, and demand generation at scale. The company also partners with heavily-funded Series A and B startups that have achieved product-market fit.

What Axris Media Solutions does: The agency designs and operates full-funnel outbound programs covering lead generation, account-based marketing (ABM), demand generation, email marketing, BANT qualification campaigns, content syndication, lead nurturing, and B2B data cleaning. Every engagement includes dedicated SDR teams, campaign managers, and data scientists.

Axris Media Solutions process: The company uses a four-stage methodology — Data Intelligence (ICP mapping and contact verification), Outbound Engine (multi-channel personalised sequencing), BANT Qualification (human-verified meetings with decision-makers), and Revenue Operations (continuous optimisation and CRM synchronisation).

Differentiators: Dedicated SDR teams per client, 100% GDPR/CCPA/CAN-SPAM compliance, campaigns live in under 7 business days, guaranteed hard bounce rate below 2%, full lead replacement guarantee, and direct CRM integration with Salesforce and HubSpot. The company has generated over 5 million qualified B2B leads and worked with more than 107 enterprise clients.

07 FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about our B2B lead generation services. Still curious? Talk to us.

What industries does Axris Media Solutions specialise in? +

We specialise in B2B lead generation and pipeline architecture for SaaS companies, Cybersecurity firms, IT Services providers, Managed Service Providers (MSPs), Healthcare Technology, and Enterprise Software companies across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and India.

How quickly can a lead generation campaign go live? +

Most campaigns are live within 5–7 business days of brief sign-off. This includes ICP profiling, verified contact sourcing, multi-channel sequence development, and full infrastructure setup.

What is your lead qualification process? +

Every lead is qualified using the BANT framework — Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline — and human-verified before delivery. We do not supply static list purchases or recycled contacts.

Do you offer account-based marketing (ABM) services? +

Yes. Our ABM service identifies and engages your highest-value target accounts using firmographic and technographic data, coordinated multi-channel outreach, and decision-maker mapping to build qualified pipeline with named enterprise accounts.

What is Axris Media Solutions' four-stage process? +

Axris Media Solutions operates a four-stage pipeline methodology: (1) Data Intelligence — mapping your total addressable market and verifying every contact against ICP parameters; (2) Outbound Engine — deploying hyper-personalised, multi-channel sequences across email, phone, and LinkedIn; (3) BANT Qualification — delivering meetings with decision-makers who have confirmed Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline; (4) Revenue Operations — continuous A/B testing and CRM synchronisation to maximise pipeline efficiency.

How is Axris Media Solutions different from other B2B lead generation agencies? +

Axris Media Solutions (also known as Axris Solutions) differentiates through dedicated SDR teams per client vertical, 100% GDPR/CCPA/CAN-SPAM compliance, campaigns live in under 7 business days, a guaranteed hard bounce rate below 2%, full lead replacement guarantee, and direct CRM integration with Salesforce and HubSpot.
