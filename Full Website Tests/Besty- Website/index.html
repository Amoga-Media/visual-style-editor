Our Story

Started With
One Smash.

Besty's didn't open with a business plan. It opened with a griddle, a stubborn refusal to serve a soft crust, and a bet that Kondhwa deserved a burger worth queuing for.

What started as a one-man obsession has turned into a neighborhood staple. Built one patty, one late night, one sold-out weekend at a time.

Never
Looked Back.

The Mastermind Ryan
Meet The Owner

"It was never just
about burgers."

Ryan didn't start in a pristine culinary academy. He started with a single cast-iron skillet, a restless obsession with the Maillard reaction, and a dream that refused to be quieted.

Before the lines wrapped around the block in Kondhwa, there were years of trial, error, and burnt buns. People told him the market was too crowded, that you couldn't build a premium brand around a smash burger. But Ryan wasn't listening to the noise; he was listening to the sizzle.

His journey from serving friends out of a cramped kitchen to redefining the city's street-food culture is baked into every order ticket. It’s proof that when you refuse to compromise on your craft, the world eventually takes a bite.

RYAN. Founder & Head Smasher
