Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

On This Page

Grand Shalimar ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website — to browse the menu, make a reservation, or sign up for our newsletter — and how we use, share, and protect it.

01

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways:

  • Reservation details — name, phone number, email, party size, date, time, and any notes you leave (allergies, occasion, seating preference).
  • Newsletter sign-up — your email address.
  • Contact requests — anything you send us by email or phone.
  • Usage data — general, non-identifying information such as browser type and pages visited, collected automatically as you browse.
02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to confirm and manage your reservation, respond to questions, send newsletter emails to those who've opted in, and understand how people use our site so we can keep improving it. We don't use your reservation details for marketing unless you've separately subscribed to our newsletter.

03

Cookies & Similar Technologies

This site may use small local storage or cookies to remember basic preferences and to understand aggregate site traffic. These aren't used to build advertising profiles. You can control or clear cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so won't prevent you from browsing the menu or making a reservation.

04

How We Share Information

We don't sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the site, send email, or manage reservations, bound to keep it confidential.
  • Authorities, if required by law or to protect the safety of our guests and staff.
  • A successor business, if Grand Shalimar Restaurant is ever sold or merged — your information would be handled under a policy at least as protective as this one.
05

Data Retention & Security

We keep reservation and contact records only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected — typically to honor your visit and follow up on anything unresolved — and remove them afterward unless you're subscribed to our newsletter. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, though no method of storage or transmission is ever 100% secure.

06

Your Rights & Choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can unsubscribe from our newsletter using the link in any email, or by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law — reach out and we'll do our best to help.

07

Children's Privacy

This website isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always show the most recent revision. We encourage you to check back occasionally.

09

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out any time.
