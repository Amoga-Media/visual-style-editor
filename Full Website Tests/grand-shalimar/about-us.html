About Us

A Kitchen And A Lawn, Both Built For Sharing

Grand Shalimar is a multi-cuisine restaurant and lawn on NIBM Road, Pune — tandoor classics, slow-cooked biryanis, and Indo-Chinese favorites for a table of two, and a lawn built for functions of 60 to 100.

The Short Version

Who We Are

We run two kitchens under one roof — a tandoor and curry kitchen turning out biryanis, kebabs, and Mughlai classics, and a Chinese kitchen doing the Indo-Chinese favorites everyone orders on repeat. Whichever you choose, it comes to the table fresh, not reheated, and there's a veg option next to every non-veg one.

Curious how the lawn fits into all of this? Read more on Our Story.

Our Standards

What Matters To Us

Ground Fresh, Every Morning

Our spice blends are mixed in-house each morning — nothing stocked in bulk months ahead.

Fire & Patience

Dum biryani sealed and slow-cooked for hours, tandoor kebabs fired over charcoal — nothing rushed to save time.

Veg & Non-Veg, Equally

Almost every non-veg dish on our menu has a veg counterpart beside it, cooked with the same care.

A Seat For Every Group

A table for two inside, or the lawn for 60 to 100 — we set up for birthdays, get-togethers, and everything between.

The Lawn

Room To Celebrate
Properly

Alongside the restaurant, our lawn is set up for the celebrations that outgrow a dining room — birthdays, engagements, get-togethers, and family functions from 60 to 100 guests, with the same kitchen behind it.

Tell us the headcount and the occasion when you reserve, and our team will call to work out the table plan, the menu spread, and everything the lawn needs to feel ready.

Before You Visit

Good To Know

Do you take walk-ins, or is a reservation required?

+

Walk-ins are always welcome, but tables turn over quickly on Fridays and Saturdays. If you'd rather not wait, book ahead and we'll have your corner of the room ready.

Can you accommodate allergies or dietary restrictions?

+

Yes — let your server know when you arrive, or leave a note in the "Special Requests" field when you reserve. Most dishes can be adapted for common allergies and vegetarian diets.

Is there parking nearby?

+

Yes — there's parking on-site for both the restaurant and the lawn, so you don't need to hunt for street parking.

Do you host private events or large groups?

+

We do — pick a party size when you reserve online, or for anything from 30 to 100+ guests on the lawn, call us directly at 083088 90889 so we can talk through the space and the spread.

Do you offer delivery or takeout?

+

Yes — order online through Zomato for delivery, or call ahead and we'll have takeout ready for pickup.
