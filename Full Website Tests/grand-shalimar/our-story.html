Our Story

Two Kitchens, One Lawn, No Shortcuts

Grand Shalimar sits on NIBM Road doing something most restaurants don't attempt twice — a full tandoor and biryani kitchen, a full Indo-Chinese kitchen, and a lawn built for the celebrations too big for either dining room.

The Process

How A Dish Gets To You

1
The Masala

Ground That Morning

Every gravy starts with a fresh spice mix made that day — not a jar labeled "ready-made" pulled off a shelf.

2
The Slow Part

Hours, Not Minutes

Biryani goes into a sealed handi for hours on dum; tandoor kebabs marinate overnight before they ever meet the charcoal.

3
Two Kitchens

Run At The Same Time

Tandoor and curries on one side, Indo-Chinese woks on the other — running in parallel so neither one waits on the other.

4
Your Table

Inside, Or On The Lawn

A quiet dinner for two indoors, or the lawn set up for the whole family — either way, it reaches you hot, not reheated.

The Standard

What We Won't Compromise On

Three things every dish has to clear before it reaches your table.

Nothing Frozen, Nothing Rushed

The tandoor is fired every morning, marinades sit overnight, and biryani gets its full hours on dum before it's ever served.

Ground In-House

Every masala is mixed fresh in our own kitchen — no pre-made pastes, no shortcuts on the spice box.

Two Cuisines, One Standard

Indian and Indo-Chinese get the same care in the kitchen — nothing on this menu is treated as the "other" menu.

Our Promise

"Whether it's a table for two or the lawn full for someone's wedding, the kitchen doesn't change gears — everyone gets the same food, cooked the same way."
