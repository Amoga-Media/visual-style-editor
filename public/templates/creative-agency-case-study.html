The Challenge

Cloud Builder Bloat & Lost Edits

The team relied on proprietary cloud platforms where exported CSS bundles ballooned to 2.4 MB. Designers made tweaks in DevTools that were instantly wiped out upon refreshing, creating a tedious loop of manual copy-paste tickets.

The Solution

Local-First Splicing & Direct Git Sync

We deployed Visual Style Editor directly across their repository. Designers now adjust padding, spring curves, and typography visually with live sliders, committing atomic diffs straight into the git repo in under 2ms.
